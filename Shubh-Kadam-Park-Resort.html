About The Resort

Luxury Hospitality In The Heart Of Nature

A thoughtfully designed hospitality development that blends luxury, comfort, recreation, and investment opportunities.

Welcome to Shubh Kadam - Jim Corbett Park & Resort, a premium hospitality development spread across 22,126 sq.ft. of thoughtfully planned space.

Designed amidst natural surroundings, the resort combines modern luxury with peaceful living, creating a destination that appeals to travelers, families, and investors alike.

The project features 18 luxury studio units and 21 spacious duplex cottages, offering a variety of stay options for different guest needs.

Whether you're looking for a hospitality investment, vacation retreat, or premium lifestyle destination, this resort delivers unmatched value and experiences.
