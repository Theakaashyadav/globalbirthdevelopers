Buyer Process

A Clear Path From Research to Site Visit

Treat every project page as a starting point. Current commercial terms, land records and site-specific facts should be confirmed before a commitment.

01

Choose a Specific Project

Compare the published property format and geography instead of treating every project as interchangeable.

02

Request Current Information

Ask for the latest price sheet, inventory, layout, payment terms and documents relevant to the exact option.

03

Inspect the Exact Location

Verify the official site pin, approach road, plot or unit identity, surroundings, utilities and current on-ground condition.

04

Review Evidence Independently

Use an independent property lawyer and relevant technical advisers to check records, permissions, costs and agreements.
