About Shubh Kadam Bhimtal

Hillside Living Near Bhimtal Lake

A peaceful residential estate blending natural charm, modern comfort, and a setting that should be assessed in person.

Shubh Kadam - Bhimtal is nestled in the serene town of Pandey Gaun, Bhimtal, District Nainital, Uttarakhand.

Spanning an impressive 137 Nali, this expansive project offers a harmonious lifestyle surrounded by lush greenery, fresh mountain air, and the calming presence of nearby lakes.

Just minutes away from the picturesque Bhimtal Lake and a short drive from Nainital city, the location offers the perfect balance between seclusion and connectivity.

Whether you want a peaceful second home, a hillside retreat, or a legacy property in the hills, Shubh Kadam - Bhimtal is a promising destination.
