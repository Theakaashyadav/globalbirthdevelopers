About Corbett Eye

Serenity, Simplicity & Soulful Living

A low-density, nature-inspired residential retreat designed for mindful living close to wildlife and nature.

Shubh Kadam - Corbett Eye, formerly known as Nirvana, is a thoughtfully designed residential retreat nestled near the enchanting Jim Corbett National Park.

Spread across 13,000 sq. ft., this elegant development features 21 well-crafted simplex cottages, offering a perfect blend of comfort, simplicity, and nature.

Corbett Eye carries forward its legacy of peaceful living with a renewed vision - minimalist yet modern homes designed to reconnect you with nature.

Wake up to birdsong, enjoy peaceful walks in the forest breeze, and experience life at a mindful pace surrounded by natural beauty.
