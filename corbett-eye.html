Before you book

A practical plot-buyer checklist

Good due diligence is more useful than a promised return. Ask for readable copies, take independent professional advice where needed, and ensure the written documents match what you see on site.

  • Identity and title: verify the seller, ownership chain, encumbrance position and plot demarcation.
  • Approvals and use: ask for land-use, layout, development and RERA registration or applicability information relevant to the project.
  • Total cost: request the base price plus registration, taxes, maintenance, club, utility and any other charges in writing.
  • Infrastructure: record the status and responsibility for roads, drainage, electricity, water, security and common amenities.
  • Buildability: confirm setbacks, height, design controls, access for construction and applicable local rules.
  • Agreement: review timelines, cancellation terms, possession wording and every promised inclusion before payment.

Ask for a current document pack

The project team can explain available plots and provide the documents relevant to the transaction. Consider having property papers reviewed by an independent local lawyer and verify government records directly.
