About Shubh Kadam Jim Corbett

Peaceful Studio Living Near Corbett

A perfect destination for eco-tourists, wildlife photographers, weekend travelers, and hospitality investors.

Shubh Kadam - Jim Corbett is a thoughtfully crafted boutique development located in the serene village of Dhela, Ramnagar, District Nainital, Uttarakhand.

Just minutes away from the iconic Jim Corbett National Park, this project is a dream come true for nature lovers, wildlife photographers, and eco-conscious travelers.

Spread across 13,616 sq.ft., Shubh Kadam features 20 fully furnished studio units, each designed to offer a peaceful stay amidst lush greenery and tranquil surroundings.

Whether you are seeking a peaceful weekend escape or looking for a smart hospitality investment, this property offers the perfect balance of natural beauty and modern convenience.
