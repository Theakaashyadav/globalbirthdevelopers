About Shubh Kadam 2.0

A Refined Mountain Living Experience

A perfect blend of nature, comfort, and modern architecture near the peaceful surroundings of Ramnagar.

Welcome to Shubh Kadam 2.0, a refined mountain living experience nestled in the serene surroundings of Village Malkhan, Ramnagar.

Spanning 20,424 sq.ft., this thoughtfully planned development features 10 meticulously crafted duplex cottages that redefine mountain living with style, space, and sustainability.

Each cottage is designed to offer a luxurious yet eco-conscious lifestyle, making it an ideal retreat for families, nature lovers, and professionals seeking peace without compromising connectivity.

Whether you are exploring a vacation home or a property near Jim Corbett National Park, Shubh Kadam 2.0 brings you close to nature while offering all the comforts of modern living.
