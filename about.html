About Globalbirth Real Estate

Globalbirth Real Estate is a renowned and trusted name in the global real estate industry. Established in 2021, our company has quickly emerged as a leading player in the market, offering a comprehensive range of real estate services to clients worldwide.

With a strong foundation built upon the expertise and experience of our owner, Dr. Narendar Pratap Singh, and our global associate status since 2004, we have established a strong presence and earned the trust of our clients.

As a global real estate company, we pride ourselves on our extensive network of associates, partners, and experts who provide valuable insights and access to premium properties in diverse markets.

We are committed to understanding the unique needs and aspirations of every client and delivering personalized solutions that exceed expectations.
