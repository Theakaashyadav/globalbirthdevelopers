Company and Location Overview

GlobalBirth Developers Private Limited publishes information about residential plotted developments and cottage, studio and leisure-led property concepts. The portfolio shown on this website spans Ramnagar and Bhimtal in Uttarakhand, together with Moradabad and the Dalpatpur–Kashipur corridor in Uttar Pradesh.

The company’s published contact address is Office No. 604/605, Advant Navis Business Park, Sector 142, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201305. This is the company office for enquiries and meetings; it is not the address of a Ramnagar, Jim Corbett, Bhimtal or Moradabad project.

Project pages are an initial information source, not a substitute for current written terms or due diligence. Buyers should confirm the exact site, available property, pricing, specifications and transaction documents for the option under consideration. View the company’s contact details.
